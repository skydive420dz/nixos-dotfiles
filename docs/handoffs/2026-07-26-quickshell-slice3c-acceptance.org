battery, and Bluetooth pollers; their state and parsing; the accepted Slice 1H

controller while =StatusCluster= retains layout, views, clicks, and its local
clock.

- =config/quickshell/shell.qml=

network and battery behavior, status geometry, click launchers, Clock, CAVA,
and the OSD transport were not redesigned.
