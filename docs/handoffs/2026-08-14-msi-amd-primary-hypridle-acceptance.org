- One normal fresh UWSM startup independently started AMD-primary Hyprland,

- Brave 4K60 playback reported ~kVideoDecoderName=VaapiVideoDecoder~ on the AMD

- Black Desert Online was observed using NVIDIA under real game load while the
  compositor remained AMD-primary.  This is partial workload evidence, not
  universal proof of the =nvidia-offload= wrapper.

* Known Limitations
- =AQ_DRM_DEVICES=/dev/dri/amd-card= excludes NVIDIA-owned display connectors
  from Aquamarine.  NVIDIA-wired HDMI, USB-C, and DisplayPort outputs and their
  HDMI/DisplayPort audio are unavailable or unvalidated in this compositor
  configuration.
- NVIDIA remains installed and the native offload wrapper remains configured,
  but isolated GLX, EGL, and Vulkan wrapper inheritance is not fully accepted.
- Fullscreen and media idle inhibition, external-display behavior,
  suspend/resume, and hotplug are not accepted.
- Design B was statically reviewed but runtime-inconclusive.  Its compositor-only
  Mesa EGL selector, =__EGL_VENDOR_LIBRARY_FILENAMES=, and UWSM service-mode
  setting, =UWSM_APP_UNIT_TYPE=service=, are deliberately not persisted.
- Fine-grained NVIDIA power management is configured, but GPU =0000:01:00.0=
  and HDA =0000:01:00.1= remained active in =D0= with =power/control=on=.
- RTD3 remains unresolved; the incomplete experiment does not establish an RTD3
  failure.  No improved battery life is claimed.
